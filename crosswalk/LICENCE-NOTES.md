The crosswalk (everything under `crosswalk/`) is licensed under

useful to people who never run the product.

## No reproduced standard text

- `requirement_summary` fields are original paraphrases written for this
  project. They are not the text of any framework document.
- ISO/IEC standards are copyrighted. When the ISO/IEC 42001 file lands
  it must reference controls by identifier with original paraphrases
  only. Do not paste standard text into this repository, in files,
  comments, tests or fixtures.
- The EU AI Act is EU law and reusable, but the same paraphrase-only
  rule is applied for consistency and to keep summaries at a stable
  reading level.

## What a mapping claims

A `supports` relationship between runtime evidence and a control means
the evidence is relevant to demonstrating the control. It never means a
legal or certification obligation is satisfied. Proofbook does not make
compliance determinations; it produces evidence that a qualified
reviewer interprets.
